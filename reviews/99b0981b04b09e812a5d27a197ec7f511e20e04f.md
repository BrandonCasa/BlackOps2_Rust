## Findings

### P1 — Fire splash bypasses burning and becomes a crawler/self-damage blast

- Draft: `src/projectiles.rs:99-105`; `src/combat_effects.rs:80-101`. Integration: `src/equipment.rs`, `resolve_blasts`, always emits `DamageCategory::Explosive` and includes the player among targets.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:225-244,265-298,318-343`, `staff_fire_zombie_hit_response_internal`, `flame_damage_fx`, `zombie_set_and_restore_flame_state`.
- Trigger/result: shoot a Fire Staff at the ground beside a zombie, or hit a zombie with others nearby. The converted 2050/3300/11500/20000 splash damage arrives as Explosive, so the new Fire branch never runs for splash victims. Survivors can instead become crawlers under the generic explosive branch; the owner also receives the newly inflated blast damage when nearby. Only the tiny native direct-hit amount retains Fire categorization. The reference applies staff fire callbacks and burning to the affected zombies; its AI-only amplification is not a player-damaging conventional explosion.
- Future correction: preserve elemental identity through blast resolution and separate original native projectile damage from zombie-only scripted elemental damage/status handling. Keep the fire eligibility rules distinct from grenade crawler conversion and player splash.

### P2 — Fire damage is constant across the radius rather than 50–100 percent

- Draft: `src/projectiles.rs:104-105`; `src/combat_effects.rs:55-61`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:232-243,265-297,403-421`.
- Trigger/result: an enemy near the outside of a Fire Staff explosion receives the full impact constant because both the native inner value 11 and outer value 1 are replaced by the same constant. Original `pct_from_center=(amount-1)/10` and `pct_damage=0.5+0.5*pct_from_center` scale both impact and burn damage: 50 percent at the edge, 100 percent at the center. The current implementation roughly doubles edge damage and changes high-round survival thresholds.
- Future correction: retain the native 1–11 interpolation signal and apply the recovered percentage to initial damage and the associated burn stream.

### P1 — The Wind Staff never executes its recovered cone attack

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:55-59,115-117`; `src/projectiles.rs:91-105`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_air.gsc:473-503`, `wind_damage_cone`.
- Trigger/result: fire the basic or uncharged upgraded Wind Staff into a group. The game definition at this revision gives these projectiles native damage 1 and explosion radius 0.0254 m. Because that radius is positive, the direct-hit branch bypasses `elemental_damage`; the amplified blast is only one inch wide. No code selects the original cone's up-to-12 nearby targets, 400-unit range, or 45/60-degree FOV. The recovered 2050/3300 numbers therefore do not deliver the expected group attack.
- Future correction: dispatch the source cone through the normal staff firing path, with its range, FOV, target cap and original special-target behavior; do not depend on the tiny native placeholder explosion to distribute scripted damage.

### P2 — Burning zombies retain melee and repeated hits restart the burn stream

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:93-100,128-141`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:270-297,318-343,403-422`.
- Trigger/result: directly hit a high-health zombie with Fire, then hit it again while burning. Draft resets the eight-second timer and one-second pulse timer, changes the burn owner, and can add an immediate next-tick pulse on every hit. It also never checks `burning` in enemy melee intent, so after the generic 0.35-second stagger the zombie can attack while burning. Original starts one burn stream only when not already on fire, waits 0.5 seconds before starting it, and disables melee until that stream ends.
- Future correction: keep one source-owned timed burn, preserve first-hit timing/ownership and repeat-impact damage separately, and apply/restores the source burning movement/melee restrictions.

### P2 — Ice is a full immobilization with no survivor cooldown

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:102-107,130-134,149`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_water.gsc:348-398`, `ice_affect_zombie`.
- Trigger/result: ice-hit a zombie that survives the delayed 2050/3300 damage. Draft sets `Zombie.stunned` for the entire 1.8–2.3 seconds, stopping movement/intent completely, then immediately permits a new freeze as soon as the timer reaches zero. Original runs the zombie at animation rate 0.3, applies delayed damage, and retains the ice state for an additional 0.5 seconds on survivors before restoring rate 1 and allowing another application. Time-to-contact and repeated-shot control differ.
- Future correction: implement the recovered slowed state and survivor recovery window. Preserve the separate 0.5–0.7-second Insta-Kill/always-kill route when that route is integrated rather than treating it as an ordinary immediate kill.

### P2 — Frozen shattering is only an unconsumed flag

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:40,127,132-134`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_water.gsc:123-200,431-442`, `freeze_zombie`, `frozen_zombie_shatter`, `staff_water_death_event`.
- Trigger/result: kill an iced zombie or kill it with the delayed ice damage. `Wounds.shattered` is set, but no changed or existing consumer uses it for corpse visibility, death animation, fragmentation or sound. The source freezes the appropriate standing/crawling death pose and performs its shatter effect/gib sequence. The promised shattering does not reach the rendered result.
- Future correction: connect an original-asset death/shatter transition to the receipt, with correct standing/crawler handling and cleanup, instead of exposing only a boolean.

### P2 — The new lightning neighbor hits neither reproduce the source area rule nor stun their victims

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:69-77,109-112`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_lightning.gsc:188-280,340-343,393-418`.
- Trigger/result: directly hit a zombie in a cluster. Draft adds up to three arbitrary query-order neighbors within 5.08 m at half the direct-hit damage, in addition to the existing native projectile explosion. These are `ImpactSource::Status` events, which `observe` skips before applying electrical stun, so the newly chained victims take damage without the new stun state. The inspected reference implements ordinary projectile damage responses and separate charged-ball target/range/pulse behavior; it does not justify this universal extra half-damage neighbor rule.
- Future correction: distinguish ordinary lightning impact from the original charged-ball arc/pulse mechanic, use its supported target/range/line-of-sight rules, and apply the proper effect state to actual secondary victims. Do not infer a universal chain rule from the checklist wording.

### P2 — Headless zombies keep their visible head and never bleed out

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:88-90`; `src/enemies.rs:1458-1463`.
- Reference: `damage-life-state-01/decompiled/t6/_zm_spawner.gsc:1241-1285,1288-1308`, `zombie_head_gib`, `damage_over_time`.
- Trigger/result: reduce a zombie to at most ten percent health with an eligible head hit. Draft disables the Head hit volume but hides only attachments whose socket contains `head` or `neck`. The original-revision game definition attaches its German head parts at `j_spine4`, so those heads remain visible but unhittable. No timed damage follows `headless=true`, leaving a headless-state zombie alive indefinitely. Original removes the attached head model and starts one damage-per-second stream using `ceil(current_health*0.2)` until death.
- Future correction: identify the actual head presentation/attachment instead of guessing from the socket name, and add the original attributed bleed-out behavior.

### P2 — Gib eligibility and crawler body state are not implemented by the limb counters

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:81-89`; `src/enemies.rs:678-679`; `src/hit_regions.rs:54-57`.
- Reference: `damage-life-state-01/decompiled/t6/_zm_spawner.gsc:1382-1498,1503-1565,1568-1611`, `zombie_gib_on_damage`, `zombie_should_gib`, `derive_damage_refs`.
- Trigger/result: any surviving explosive hit of at least ten percent health with region None becomes a crawler, regardless of explosion location or selected limb. The limb totals are never consumed; no arm threshold/removal exists, and crawler conversion only scales upright movement by 0.2 without changing stance, model, collision or leg hit volumes. Draft also permits a low-health head removal on melee, whereas source explicitly rejects melee gibbing. Original derives actual limb references from hit location/nearest tags, applies eligible weapon/damage exclusions, and changes leg-loss stance/physics.
- Future correction: implement or explicitly defer the actual limb-selection/removal contract; update crawler presentation, collision, hit volumes and locomotion together, and preserve the source damage-type exclusions.

### P2 — Panzer armor skips helmet damage from small hits and omits shotgun modifiers

- Draft: `src/combat_effects.rs:14-27`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_ai_mechz.gsc:1464-1546`, `mechz_damage_override`.
- Trigger/result: a <=10-damage head pellet passes through at full damage but returns before adding any helmet damage. Source changes the damage percentage for small hits and still processes helmet accumulation. Shotguns also never receive the recovered 1.5 multiplier for body damage or exposed-head damage. Once the next commit activates this component on Panzers, pellet weapons and weak-helmet damage thresholds diverge from the original.
- Future correction: compute the source multipliers without bypassing armor-state updates; supply weapon-class and powerup context to the reusable resolver. The additional Panzer-specific weak-point and powerup differences are recorded in the Panzer commit report.
