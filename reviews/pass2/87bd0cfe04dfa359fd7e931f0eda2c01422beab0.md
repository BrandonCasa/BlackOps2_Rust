Correction: extend the existing blast request with explicit player damage/falloff and preserved weapon identity; route source player-damage exclusions before applying health, retaining enemy falloff separately. Confidence high; current at the endpoint. Later elemental work does not repair this root; see the elemental report for loss of Fire damage category.
