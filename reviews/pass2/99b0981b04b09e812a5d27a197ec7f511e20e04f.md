## Actionable correctness findings

### P1  -  Preserve Fire identity through splash resolution

Trigger: Fire Staff splash hits a zombie without a direct projectile hit. `src/projectiles.rs:99-105` puts converted elemental amounts in BlastRequest, but `src/equipment.rs::resolve_blasts` emits Explosive. `src/combat_effects.rs:80-101` consequently applies crawler handling and never starts the Fire burn. `_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:225-244,265-298,318-343` calls Fire hit response, burn and temporary melee-state restoration. Draft loses the defining burn effect and can create explosive crawlers.

Correction: carry damage category/weapon callback identity in the existing blast request and dispatch Fire hit response once per affected actor. Player immunity is the earlier shared-blast defect owned by `reviews/pass2/87bd0cfe04dfa359fd7e931f0eda2c01422beab0.md`; do not duplicate its fix here. Confidence high; current.

### P1  -  Execute the Wind Staff cone

Trigger: fire Wind at a group spanning the authored cone. `src/combat_effects.rs:55-59,115-117` changes hit amount/knockback but `src/projectiles.rs:91-105` uses a single projectile impact/tiny native blast. `_zm_weap_staff_air.gsc:473-503` selects the cone's multiple visible targets with source range/dot thresholds and applies wind damage. Draft misses targets and changes crowd control.

Correction: implement a cone target request from the shot's origin/direction using source range/angle/visibility and normal DamageQueue/knockback, avoiding replacement of source cone width with a projectile-radius sphere. Confidence high; current.

### P2  -  Retain Fire radial multiplier

Trigger: splash near the edge rather than center. `src/projectiles.rs:104-105` converts both native endpoints to the same 2050/3300/etc.; `src/combat_effects.rs:55-61` erases native 1-11 radial information. `_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:232-243,265-297,403-421` derives the 0.5-1 source multiplier. Draft delivers full effect across the radius.

Correction: retain raw native splash interpolation or explicitly pass the derived multiplier into the Fire hit response; apply it before rounding the source damage/burn amount. Confidence high; current.

### P2  -  Do not restart an active burn or leave melee enabled

Trigger: repeatedly strike a burning survivor. `src/combat_effects.rs:93-100,128-141` resets duration, first-tick timer and owner on every hit and does not suppress melee. `_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:270-297,318-343,403-422` guards an active burn stream, disables melee during it and restores prior state afterward. Draft changes tick cadence, kill attribution and threat.

Correction: represent burn as one owned state with source re-hit rules, restore saved melee eligibility on exit and keep the timer/attribution stable while active. Confidence high; current.

### P2  -  Use Ice slow/recovery rather than complete stun

Correction: add an Ice movement-scale/cooldown component consumed by normal enemy movement and damage eligibility, restore prior scale at exit and preserve source random duration consumption at first accepted application. Confidence high; current.
