## Actionable correctness findings

### P1  -  Apply the executed shield fling damage and target test

Trigger: bash an ordinary nearby zombie. `src/buildables.rs:90-97` uses 75 Melee damage and 0.8-second stun with a yaw-only 0.4-dot cutoff. Source `_zm_weap_riotshield_tomb.gsc:388-414,454-531,548-586` tests the actual forward hemisphere/eye-centroid range and cone visibility; within the 90-unit fling range it applies 2500 impact damage and fling/knockdown behavior. Survivors and shield wear are therefore substantially wrong, especially in early rounds.

Correction: retain source target classification and one dedicated shield impact path, including per-affected-target wear and killed ragdoll response, using normal DamageQueue and Avian visibility checks. Confidence high, current.

### P2  -  Raising a shield must interrupt the firearm action

Trigger: raise during a queued burst/reload or reload/cycle afterward. `src/buildables.rs:55-67,82-89` toggles a flag and masks only fire/melee/ADS; the existing burst action continues emitting at `src/weapons.rs:990-1007`. Source `_zm_riotshield_tomb.gsc:195-242` switches to the shield weapon; `_zm_weap_riotshield_tomb.gsc:364-372` bashes from its actual melee event. Draft permits firearm use behind front protection and leaves the gun presentation active.

Correction: represent shield raising as the existing temporary equipment weapon/action state, cancel incompatible pending emissions, route reload/cycle/slot inputs consistently and restore the gun on stow. Confidence high, current.

### P2  -  Preserve independent shield and drone activation

Trigger: take a drone while owning a shield, then deploy it. `src/crafting.rs:144-150` overwrites the single `equipped` key; `src/buildables.rs:55-79` clears it on drone launch. The shield stays owned but is unreachable through that activation switch, and duplicate ownership blocks bench reacquisition. Source `zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:1068-1075` assigns the drone its separate action slot; shield action ownership is separate in `_zm_riotshield_tomb.gsc:195-242`.

Correction: use separate activation slots/state for shield and drone, or an explicit reachable selection path preserving every owned buildable after deployment. Confidence high, current.

### P2  -  Validate shield placement before consuming it
