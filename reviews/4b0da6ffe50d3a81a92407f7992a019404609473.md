## P2 - The charger cannot replenish an upgraded staff

- Original code: `src/staff_upgrades.rs:101-114`; `src/crafting.rs:160-166`; shared `src/quests.rs:138-148`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_main_quest.gsc:1208-1255` repeatedly accepts the upgraded staff, retains its loaded/reserve ammunition, and adds `int(max_ammo / 20)` stock per arriving soul, with early retrieval after ammunition starts arriving. `solo-content/source/weapons/staff_fire_upgraded_zm` and the other upgraded staff definitions supply the native ammunition capacities.
- Trigger and actual behavior: retrieve an upgraded staff, expend its ammunition, then use its Crazy Place charger. The insertion predicate only accepts the basic `WEAPONS[index]` ID. An upgraded staff is rejected despite the insertion prompt. Furthermore, the collector remains at its completed twenty-soul count, so accepting that ID alone would still not restart collection.
- Gameplay impact: the source's soul-powered ammunition replenishment loop is unavailable after the initial upgrade.
- Future correction: distinguish initial upgrade charging from upgraded-staff ammunition charging; preserve the deposited staff's magazine/reserve and reactivate a suitable soul receiver with source per-soul stock and retrieval rules.
- Status: outstanding at the bounded tip; neither later commit changes this insertion/collector lifecycle.
