## P2 - Basic staffs receive upgraded staff melee damage

- Draft: `src/weapons.rs:202-205,813-815`; `src/gameplay.rs:266-270`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_main_quest.gsc:94-97` assigns elemental melee weapons to upgraded staff metadata only. `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_utility.gsc:1658-1685` defaults to knife and selects staff melee only when the relevant staff metadata defines it. Flat definitions `solo-content/source/weapons/staff_fire_zm` and other basic staff files have `meleeDamage=25`; the separate `staff_*_melee_zm` files have `meleeDamage=1700`, `meleeDelay=0.25`, `meleeTime=0.7`.
- Trigger: craft any basic staff and perform melee before upgrading it or obtaining One Inch Punch.
- Actual: melee_definition recognizes every staff family regardless of upgrade state, then gameplay always assigns 1700 damage whenever that lookup succeeds.
- Impact: basic staff possession grants the upgraded melee attack prematurely, changing early-round combat and the value of staff upgrades.
- Future correction: choose the accessory melee from the actual base/upgraded staff state and preserve the source knife fallback, rather than infer entitlement from the name prefix alone.

## P2 - Returning from Sekhmet mode has no equip/recovery interval

- Draft: `src/weapons.rs:207-215,218-221`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_utility.gsc:609-660,1705-1715` exposes Sekhmet as a separate weapon selection while retaining the staff. Flat `solo-content/source/weapons/staff_revive_zm` and upgraded elemental definitions provide native equip/unequip animation/timing fields.
- Trigger: fire/reload Sekhmet, then switch back to the elemental staff as soon as Ready is reached.
- Actual: entering Sekhmet schedules Equipping, but returning restores the saved slot, calls cancel_action and is immediately Ready. The staff can start charging/firing in the same update without its equip action.
- Impact: mode switching cancels expected weapon recovery/raise timing in one direction and produces asymmetric handling.
- Future correction: model both selections through the normal weapon switch/equip lifecycle while preserving each mode's magazine and reserve.

## P2 - Sekhmet ammunition is shared across distinct elemental staffs

- Draft: `src/weapons.rs:69-72,96-98,207-220,319-322,370,494-501`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:1281-1284` stores revive stock/clip on the particular upgraded staff object; `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_utility.gsc:1705-1715` restores that staff's saved values when its revive weapon is given. `solo-content/source/weapons/staff_revive_zm` defines `startAmmo=3`, `maxAmmo=3`, `clipSize=1`.
- Trigger: spend the revive ammunition of one upgraded staff, exchange/drop it, then take a different upgraded staff.
- Actual: one inventory-wide revive_magazine and one permanent `ammo.staff_revive` pool serve every element. exchange_staff restores the mode but neither associates nor saves revive ammunition per staff; a newly taken staff inherits the previous staff's depleted loaded/reserve state.
- Impact: one staff's usage changes another staff's Sekhmet ammunition and discarded/retrieved staff ammunition cannot follow its original owner/state.
- Future correction: retain revive magazine/reserve alongside each persistent staff state and initialize a newly available staff from its native ammo definition. The separate elemental/revive pool intent is correct; its lifetime/ownership is too broad.

## P2 - Fire field pulses omit the source impact damage and repeatedly reset burn timing

- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:129-145` applies flame_damage_fx to nearby targets each 0.2-second area iteration. `:266-297,346-357` applies 11500/20000 impact damage for tier two/three, while `:403-422` keeps the separate 300/450-per-second burn loop.
- Trigger: a zombie enters a lingering charged Fire field after the original projectile blast has passed, or remains in it.

## Evidence gaps and reviewed integration

Inspected every changed Rust hunk and the original weapon inventory, offhand cancellation, projectile/blast integration, elemental effect observer, native staff weapon fields and GSC field lifecycles. The native charge-tier chain is confirmed by each upgraded weapon's `fireType=Charge Shot` and `altWeapon` links. The inspected flat dumps and GSC do not specify authoritative engine charge thresholds or per-tier ammo debit constants: GSC observes `chargeshotlevel` rather than defining it (`todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_utility.gsc:664-699`). Consequently the draft one/two-second thresholds and Wind [1,2,3]/other [1,3,6] debits at `src/weapons.rs:1031-1039` remain explicit fidelity evidence gaps, not asserted numerical mismatches. Native `freezeMovementWhenFiring=0` does not justify inventing a movement freeze requirement while charging.

At this original revision, basic staffs already have `.upgraded` links in game.json and unchanged Pack-a-Punch handling (`src/stations.rs:199-223`) accepts them. Therefore the new upgraded branches are accessible through that existing shortcut. The missing original staff-upgrade quest and source-inaccurate shortcut predate this commit and are not attributed to it here. Native Sekhmet clip/reserve values are present; ammo ownership/restoration and actual revive behavior are the separate issues above. Field FX/model binding is also absent from ElementalField construction; this static review makes no visual/runtime acceptance claim. Projectile player self-damage is covered by the earlier projectile review and is intentionally not duplicated here.
