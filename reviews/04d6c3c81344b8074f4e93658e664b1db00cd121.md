## Findings

### P1 — Generators drain while attackers are still emerging, travelling, blocked or distracted

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:45,62-73`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:1022-1074,1266-1299,1664-1676,1930-1940`, `init_recapture_zombie`, `recapture_zombie_poi_think`, `get_progress_rate`, `get_recapture_attacker_count`.
- Trigger/result: leave any attacker alive away from the selected generator, or lure all attackers away while defending. Draft reduces progress by one fortieth per second whenever any raid actor is alive; it never uses the queried attacker Transform or checks arrival, melee intent, readiness or active lure. A generator therefore shuts off after forty seconds even if nobody reaches it. Source marks an attacker only after reaching its attack point within 50 native units, clears that state when distracted, and uses only actual attackers for decay. Once an attacked generator has zero current attackers, source restores progress over ten seconds.
- Future correction: track actual generator attack participation from arrival/attack state, suspend decay during travel/distraction, and restore the contested generator when its attackers disengage. Do not use global raid population as the attack condition.

### P2 — Raid zombies are unintentionally immune to every new elemental wound/status effect

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:85-90`; integration in `src/combat_effects.rs:79-80`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:976-1002,1022-1032`; `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_staff_water.gsc:348-398`; `_zm_weap_staff_fire.gsc:225-243,265-298`; `_zm_weap_staff_lightning.gsc:393-418` in the same reference directory.
- Trigger/result: directly hit a raid crusader with Ice or Fire. Actors are classified as `PopulationKind::GeneratorDefender`, and the preceding combat-effects observer rejects every non-Ordinary target. Native one-damage Ice impacts never receive delayed 2050/3300 damage or freezing; Fire direct hits never burn; electrical/wind stun is also skipped. Reference recapture zombies exclude leg-loss gib references, not all elemental effects; the staff implementations explicitly special-case mechs rather than recapture crusaders.
- Future correction: distinguish recapture-specific no-crawler behavior from valid elemental damage/status eligibility, rather than tying all wound handling to round-budget population classification.

### P2 — Raids can start midway through a round instead of at the source round boundary

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:36-42`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:2139-2158`, `recapture_round_tracker`.
- Trigger/result: reach round ten with only three powered generators, then finish capturing the fourth midway through combat. Draft immediately rolls a raid on the next fixed tick; similarly, a temporarily busy capture can defer a due raid only until that capture ends. Original waits for `between_round_over` (or an explicit debug force event) before evaluating the round, capture-busy state and four-generator requirement. If those conditions fail, it waits for another boundary.
- Future correction: consume the normal round-start/between-round event and evaluate eligibility once there. Preserve the recovered random three-to-five-round subsequent gap independently of when actors finish spawning.

### P2 — Recapture crusaders run instead of sprinting

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:87`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:1022-1029`, `init_recapture_zombie`.
- Trigger/result: observe raid actors moving to the first or next generator. Draft constructs them with `Gait::Run` and the run speed, while source explicitly sets `zombie_move_speed="sprint"`. Their travel time, pursuit animation and interception window differ, especially after moving between distant generators.
- Future correction: use the source sprint gait/speed for recapture actors while preserving separate initial-capture defender rules.

### P2 — Attack-point placement ignores generator orientation and the recovered accessible point set

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:62-65`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:246-260,601-620,1083-1159`, `setup_inaccessible_zombie_attack_points`, `setup_zombie_attack_points`, `get_unclaimed_attack_point`.
- Trigger/result: attack a generator rotated relative to world axes, or one with source-blocked access points. Draft uses four world-axis offsets 1.3208 m from the center, without the generator's yaw, accessibility exclusions or outer-pillar points. Source creates twelve points around three generator anchors in generator-relative forward/right directions, excludes named inaccessible points at the start bunker/tank trench, and assigns unclaimed valid points. Draft can route actors into solid generator geometry or to a side they should not use.
- Future correction: derive the recovered local point set from the placement transform, keep the accessibility mask, claim/release valid slots, and verify actual reachability before attack participation.

### P2 — Max Ammo is awarded without the source 'generator under attack' condition

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:94-101`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:2376-2404`, `recapture_zombie_death_func`, `drop_max_ammo_at_death_location`.
- Trigger/result: kill all four actors before they reach their generator, or kill the final survivor while it is travelling between generators. Draft only requires the current target still be powered; it has no actual-under-attack state. Source drops Max Ammo only when the final recapture zombie dies while `generator_under_attack` is set. It also ground-traces the last death position before creating the drop, whereas draft sends the actor center directly to a digging-specific rising pickup helper.
- Future correction: use the real attack-state condition and source-grounded drop location, with the appropriate ordinary powerup spawn presentation/lifetime instead of dig-emergence behavior.

### P2 — Capture rewards bypass the original challenge chests and item presentation

- Draft: `src/generators.rs:546-557`; integration: `src/environment/digging.rs:1030-1069`, `src/crafting.rs:170-174`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_utility.gsc:1475-1496`, `zone_capture_powerup`; `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_main_quest.gsc:782-828`, `staff_unlock_with_zone_capture`, `reward_staff_piece`.
- Trigger/result: complete the first generator or generator six. Draft immediately spawns a permanent automatic Double Points pickup at the start reward box location and an invisible Fire Staff target at the church reward location. Source waits two seconds after the first capture finishes, opens the start-bunker challenge box with the solo Double Points reward, and opens the church-capture chest for a modeled/glowing Fire Staff tip with its rise-and-grab sequence. The invisible marker problem comes from the new preceding-commit `reveal_part` helper; this commit newly uses it for a chest reward.
- Future correction: use the recovered chest unlock/claim flow, original part/powerup asset presentation and timing, while preserving one-time eligibility. The multiplayer Zombie Blood alternative is outside the draft's current solo scope.

### P2 — Players receive no source raid-start/travel/under-attack warning

- Draft: `src/generators/raids.rs:36-42,51-72`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_capture_zones.gsc:1718-1775,2225-2268,2407-2418`, `hide_zone_objective_while_recapture_group_runs_to_next_generator`, `recapture_zombie_group_icon_show`, `generator_under_attack_warnings`.
- Trigger/result: a raid begins at a distant generator or survivors move to the next target. Draft changes private raid/target state and only emits a stop sound after shutdown; there is no source warning loop/flare or travelling-group objective indicator tied to these state transitions. Original starts raid audio, marks the moving group while no generator is under attack, and plays a losing-generator alarm/flare once attackers arrive. Without this information the player cannot locate and respond to the event as intended.
- Future correction: connect the real raid and attack transitions to recovered HUD/objective and audio/flare assets, including stop/retarget cleanup. Establish actual attack participation first so warnings reflect observable danger.
