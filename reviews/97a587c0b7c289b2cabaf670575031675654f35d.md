## P2 - Return routes unlock before their elemental entrance has ever opened

- Draft: `src/portals.rs:33-36,122-130`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_teleporter.gsc:212-216` (`run_chamber_entrance_teleporter`) creates `run_chamber_exit` only once that element has completed its first opening; `:114-123` creates/shows the matching return frame and activation trigger.
- Trigger: enter the Crazy Place through one elemental tunnel, then approach another element's return location without having opened that entrance.
- Actual: setup creates all four usable returns unconditionally, and the return arm has no per-element unlock check. It can open and transport the player to an unopened tunnel.
- Impact: route access and the need to obtain/use the other records differ from the reference.
- Future correction: track first completed entrance opening separately from crystal visitation and expose each return only when its corresponding entrance has unlocked it. Retain that return after gramophone retrieval, as the source does.

## P2 - Open portals require another aimed use action instead of radius entry

- Draft: `src/portals.rs:73-78,113-130`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_teleporter.gsc:185,259-275` uses a passive entrance radius; `:231-255` automatically teleports players within the open return radius, excluding prone/teleporting players. `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_utility.gsc:825-840` confirms omitted `use_trigger` means passive radius, while `run_chamber_exit:122` uses a separate use trigger only for opening.
- Trigger: walk into an open entrance/return, look away, reload inside it, or approach it prone.
- Actual: every travel action needs line of sight, a forward dot above 0.7, a fresh use press, and a Ready weapon/mobility state. Conversely there is no prone exclusion.
- Impact: players can stand inside active portals without travelling, cannot travel while reloading, and can travel prone; the activation/travel distinction is lost.
- Future correction: keep use interaction for gramophone placement and return construction, then perform open-portal travel through the reference proximity/stance conditions.

## P2 - Teleport cooldown does not provide the source transit protection or control lock

- Draft: `src/portals.rs:58-62,115-118,129-130`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_teleporter.gsc:309-382` (`stargate_teleport_player`) sets teleporting, disables weapons/offhands, freezes controls, moves into a separate teleport room for two seconds, then restores controls at the destination.
- Trigger: enter a portal while enemies threaten the destination, or hold movement/fire during travel.
- Actual: relocation is immediate and the two-second cooldown only rejects further portal use. Weapons, offhands, movement and hostile interactions continue at the destination during the interval that the original spends in its transit room.
- Impact: immediate destination damage/fire and movement alter survivability and travel timing.
- Future correction: add a reusable transit state with the source duration and control/offhand restrictions, staging the player away from combat until arrival. Presentation can be refined separately.

## P3 - Travel retains the departure view angles

- Draft: `src/portals.rs:58-62,115-116,129`; `src/portals/source.rs:14-25`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_teleporter.gsc:367-378` selects a destination struct then applies both its origin and angles.

## P2 - Entering last stand prematurely removes the player's crystal

- Draft: `src/crafting.rs:183-190`, especially the `Standing` exclusion at `:185`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_craftables.gsc:308-312` (`player_drop_piece_on_downed`) waits for `bled_out` before releasing the piece. The crystal-specific callback at `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:544-556` then returns its model/trigger to the original plinth.
- Trigger: enter last stand while carrying an unconsumed elemental crystal, then recover through Quick Revive before bleeding out.
- Actual: every non-Standing state clears the ownership entry and recreates the pickup immediately, including temporary Downed. The player recovers without the crystal. The reference retains the carried piece during last stand and releases it only after bleedout, or on disconnect through the separate release path.
- Impact: a recoverable down interrupts staff assembly and forces another Crazy Place collection trip that the source does not require.
- Future correction: retain crystal ownership throughout last stand and release only at the source terminal/disconnect transition. Returning a released crystal to its original plinth is correct; the timing is the discrepancy.
## P2 - A dropped crystal is recreated without its visible model

- Draft: `src/crafting.rs:183-190` and the called helper at `:176-180`; compare initial model attachment at `:58-65`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:544-556` (`ondrop_crystal`) moves the existing crystal model back to its original plinth, shows it and re-enables its trigger.
- Trigger: bleed out while owning an unconsumed elemental gem, then return to recover it. The draft also executes this path prematurely on an ordinary down, as recorded separately above.
- Actual: recovery removes its ownership record and calls `reveal_part`, which creates only a transform/visibility/interaction entity. The previously collected entity was despawned; unlike initial setup, this path attaches no WorldAssetRoot or scene.
- Impact: the crystal remains interactable but is invisible, obscuring required staff progression after its release.
- Future correction: reuse a common crystal spawn path that restores its presentation and pickup state together.

## P3 - Crystal availability skips the reference emergence sequence

- Draft: `src/crafting.rs:58-65,89-92`; `src/portals.rs:116-117`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_main_quest.gsc:551-602` (`staff_crystal_wait_for_teleport`) initially hides the crystal below the map, waits for the matching entrance traversal, moves its plinth for six seconds, requires a continuous one-second look, then reveals/rises/rotates the crystal.
- Trigger: collect the matching crystal immediately after travel.
- Actual: `visited` suppresses only pickup candidacy, then enables pickup immediately on relocation. There is no plinth-rise, continuous-look or emergence state. Initial model lookup is a separate pre-existing crafting issue; this finding does not assume that lookup succeeds.
- Impact: the source reveal timing and look requirement disappear, allowing immediate collection.
- Future correction: separate hidden, emerging and available crystal states, applying the source visitation and look conditions before enabling presentation/pickup.
