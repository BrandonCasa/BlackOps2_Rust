## P2 - Shootable hatches close on a timer starting at the shot

- Draft: `src/robots.rs:61-64,88,100,112`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_giant_robot.gsc:414-441` opens the selected sole after damage. `:678-698,444-447` closes it five seconds after a player has entered and the stomp kill trigger ends, rather than five seconds after the shot.
- Trigger: shoot the eligible foot early in its eight-second descent, or open a foot and wait for its next eligible step without entering.
- Actual: hatch_open immediately becomes five seconds, counts down, and is reset after every stomp even if nobody entered. An early valid shot can expire before the same foot reaches the player.
- Impact: a successfully opened hatch can still crush the player; open state persistence through the walk cycle differs from the source.
- Future correction: track the selected sole's persistent closed/open state and close it in response to completed entry with the reference delay, independently of route-step timing.

## P3 - Ordinary robot selection changes the source probability and repetition rules
