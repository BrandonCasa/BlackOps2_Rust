## Findings

### P2 - Grant a collected free-perk powerup without requiring a second pickup and drink

- **Code:** `src/powerups.rs:358-360,415-416`; `src/perks/vendors.rs:151-158,160-173`; integration `src/perks.rs:42-62,161-170` and its downed-drink cancellation.
- **Trigger:** Collect a configured `powerup.random_perk` while eligible, then move away, allow its replacement bottle to expire, or become downed before the resulting drink activates.
- **Draft behavior:** Collection consumes the original powerup and acknowledges it, but only creates a separate 30-second ground bottle. The perk is granted later, after collecting that second entity and completing PerkDrinking. The reward can therefore be lost after the powerup has already been collected and announced.
- **Reference:** `damage-life-state-01/decompiled/t6/_zm_powerups.gsc:1976-1999` calls `give_random_perk` directly for eligible standing nonspectators. `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:2515-2544` selects and immediately calls `give_perk`; it does not create a second collectible or start a purchase drink.
- **Impact:** Acquisition timing, weapon interruption, failure opportunities, and the final granted outcome differ from the native free-perk powerup. The optional helper's stated drinking design explains the draft behavior but does not establish source equivalence.
- **Future correction:** Keep a generic ground-bottle helper if useful, but route the native free-perk powerup through its immediate source grant semantics. Do not conflate this reward with an Origins red-dig purchase-slot bottle.

### P2 - Derive the free-perk pool from its source acquisition route

- **Code:** `src/perks/vendors.rs:144-150`; new caller `src/powerups.rs:358-360`.
- **Trigger:** Enable a free-perk powerup while an unowned Wunderfizz-only perk exists in the full content catalog.
- **Draft behavior:** The helper samples all supported unowned perks with drink definitions. In the committed catalog that includes nine entries, including Double Tap, Deadshot, Electric Cherry, and PhD Flopper, without consulting which source vending triggers exist.
- **Reference:** `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:2518-2532` builds the free-perk pool from `zombie_vending` triggers, excluding Pack-a-Punch. `:2842-2889` creates those triggers from eligible machine placements. `game-prepare/entities/maps/mp/zm_tomb.d3dbsp.ents:27360-27435` supplies the five regular Origins perk machines plus Pack-a-Punch. The separate Wunderfizz pool is populated in `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb.gsc:1191-1202` and stored/selected by `zombies/_zm_perk_random.gsc:486-516`; its larger pool does not automatically become the free-perk vending-trigger pool.
- **Impact:** Selecting from the global catalog gives rewards unavailable through this source route and changes the distribution of all remaining candidates. A new catalog perk can silently change this reward even when no native vending machine exists for it.
- **Future correction:** Represent the eligible pool for each acquisition route explicitly. Preserve free-perk vending eligibility and Wunderfizz selection as separate rules rather than assuming every supported catalog perk is interchangeable.

### P2 - Exclude a perk already being purchased or drunk from random selection

- **Code:** `src/perks/vendors.rs:144-150`; `src/powerups.rs:326-337,358-360`; integration `src/perks.rs:26-39,161-170`.
- **Trigger:** Collect a free-perk powerup while PerkDrinking is in progress, before that selected perk enters `PerkInventory.owned`.
- **Draft behavior:** Candidate filtering reads only owned perks and solo-revive eligibility. The powerup query does not read the in-flight PerkDrinking selection, so random selection may choose that same perk. Once the first drink grants it, `ground_bottles` rejects the redundant bottle because the perk is owned. The original powerup was already consumed, leaving an unusable duplicate reward until expiry. The new per-tick HashSet prevents multiple ground-bottle drinks being queued in one invocation; it does not reserve an already in-flight perk against this selector.
- **Reference:** `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:2525-2532` explicitly excludes `self.perk_purchased` as well as owned and paused perks before choosing the reward.
- **Impact:** Normal overlapping acquisition paths can consume the free-perk reward without adding a different perk. This remains a selection defect even if the immediate-grant behavior above is corrected.
- **Future correction:** Supply the source-equivalent pending acquisition state to selection and exclude reserved/in-flight and paused perks as applicable. Make selection and grant agree about eligibility.

### P3 - Use the prepared world bottle presentation for a ground item

- **Code:** `src/perks/vendors.rs:153-156`.
- **Trigger:** Spawn a random bottle using any prepared perk bottle weapon whose first-person and pickup scenes differ.
- **Draft behavior:** The ground entity resolves `weapon.presentation`, which is the first-person presentation, rather than the existing `weapon.pickup` world presentation. The committed Juggernog bottle, for example, has distinct `actor.weapon_c9fe872bc6fc501905010e30` and `actor.weapon_119ca4f4471529dc03e0517d` scenes for those roles.
- **Reference:** `solo-content/source/weapons/zombie_perk_bottle_jugg` distinguishes `gunModel=t6_wpn_zmb_perk_bottle_jugg_view` from `worldModel=t6_wpn_zmb_perk_bottle_jugg_world`. The recovered and prepared content already preserves this distinction; no replacement art is necessary.
- **Impact:** The reusable ground item displays first-person geometry with the wrong world role/origin instead of the source world bottle. This concerns the helper's presentation independently of whether native free-perk collection should use the helper at all.
- **Future correction:** Resolve the prepared pickup/world presentation for physical ground bottles and retain the first-person presentation for the drink action.
