## Findings

### P2 — Fire Sale purchases advance the teddy counter, and ordinary thresholds are one purchase early

- Draft: `src/stations.rs:177-181`, with reset at `src/stations.rs:103-107`.
- Reference: `mechanics-economy-01/decompiled/t6/_zm_magicbox.gsc:1135-1201` (`treasure_chest_weapon_spawn`) and `:598-599` (increment after the completed offer, excluding Fire Sale).
- Trigger: buy the fourth normal box, the eighth normal box before its first move, or several discounted Fire Sale boxes followed by a normal box.
- Current behavior: increments `uses` before checking the current roll and increments it for every Fire Sale location. The fourth purchase can be a teddy and the eighth is forced to be one; sale purchases can force the next normal box to move.
- Reference behavior: the roll checks the number of prior completed normal offers, then increments after the offer. Fire Sale offers do not increment that counter. The first four ordinary offers are therefore protected, and the forced first move is evaluated after eight prior offers.
- Impact: fewer guaranteed weapon purchases and unintended early relocation/refunds after a Fire Sale.
- Correction direction: distinguish completed normal offers from purchases, exclude sale offers, and evaluate teddy thresholds against the pre-offer count. Preserve the distinction through collection and expiry.
- Tip status: still present in the reviewed tip station code.

### P2 — Ray Gun and Mark II can be owned together, and Mark II selection skips Origins' additional rejection

- Draft: `src/stations.rs:117-125`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb.gsc:1616-1632` (`tomb_special_weapon_magicbox_check`), and `:1157-1163` (Mark II quota).
- Trigger: roll while owning either base/upgraded Ray Gun family; alternatively roll with neither family owned.
- Current behavior: filters only the candidate's own base/upgraded ID. The other Ray Gun family remains in the pool, and Mark II has the same base weight as an ordinary gun.
- Reference behavior: owning either Ray Gun family excludes the other. A Mark II candidate additionally fails the map-specific check when `randomint(100) >= 33`; only one Mark II can be in circulation.
- Impact: an unavailable dual-Ray-Gun loadout becomes possible and Mark II acquisition probability changes substantially.
- Correction direction: implement the map-specific family exclusion and candidate rejection within the source-equivalent weighted selection process; track the global limited-weapon claim for any later multiplayer support.
- Tip status: still present.

### P2 — Already upgraded weapons cannot use Origins' 2000-point attachment repack

- Draft: `src/stations.rs:200-207`, `:218-223`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb.gsc:167` enables `zombiemode_reusing_pack_a_punch`; `mechanics-economy-02/decompiled/t6/_zm_weapons.gsc:1757-1813` (`get_upgrade_weapon`, `can_upgrade_weapon`, `will_upgrade_weapon_as_attachment`); `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:583-592`, `:682-705`, and `:778-790` preserve ammo for the attachment transaction.
- Trigger: insert a previously upgraded weapon that supports Pack-a-Punch attachments.
- Current behavior: requires the active definition's `upgraded` field to point to another weapon and always charges 5000. Upgraded catalog definitions normally have no next upgrade, so the transaction is rejected. There is no attachment choice or preserved-ammunition repack path.
- Reference behavior: supported upgraded guns can be repacked for 2000 to change attachments, preserving ammunition rather than giving a fresh full refill.
- Impact: the repeat Pack-a-Punch feature is unavailable despite the map enabling it.
- Correction direction: represent a separate attachment transaction with the supported source table, cost, and saved-ammo outcome; keep it separate from the first 5000-point upgrade.
- Tip status: still present.

### P2 — A power interruption preserves a deposited weapon for later collection

- Draft: `src/stations.rs:108-114`, `:197-199`.
- Reference: `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:620`, `:709-718`, `:833-853` (`destroy_weapon_in_blackout`).
- Trigger: lose a generator while the Pack-a-Punch transaction is processing or its weapon is ready, then restore power before the draft's ready timer expires.
- Current behavior: power is checked only by interaction. The independent timer continues to produce/retain `UpgradePhase::Ready`, so restoring all six generators allows collection of the same deposit.
- Reference behavior: the machine attaches a blackout watcher to the deposit; `Pack_A_Punch_off` ends collection and destroys the world gun, including its paired model if dual wielded.
- Impact: generator attacks no longer impose the original risk of losing the weapon being upgraded.
- Correction direction: connect power loss to the transaction state and terminate/destroy the deposited reward in the same cases as the reference. Do not merely hide its prompt until power returns.
- Tip status: still present.

### P2 — Pack-a-Punch deposit skips the temporary hand action

- Draft: `src/stations.rs:207-213`; `src/weapons.rs:287-295` (`deposit_active`).
- Reference: `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:629-632` and `:857-912` (`do_knuckle_crack`, `upgrade_knuckle_crack_begin`, `upgrade_knuckle_crack_end`).
- Trigger: upgrade one gun while retaining another usable gun.
- Current behavior: immediately removes the deposited slot, changes active index to another gun, and calls `cancel_action`, leaving that gun ready during processing. No knuckle-crack/hand-busy state is created.
- Reference behavior: equips the temporary Pack-a-Punch hand weapon, increments the drinking/action lock, disables player move states, and restores the prior weapon state when the temporary action ends or is interrupted.
- Impact: the player can fire immediately after depositing instead of being vulnerable during the source hand action, and the expected first-person transaction animation is absent.
- Correction direction: implement a reusable temporary action state and restoration/cancellation path for deposit, rather than treating deposit as an instantaneous inventory edit.
- Tip status: still present.

### P3 — Reloading blocks timed weapon collection even though the receiving inventory supports it

- Draft: `src/stations.rs:164`, `:188-190`, `:221`.
- Reference: `mechanics-economy-01/decompiled/t6/_zm_magicbox.gsc:673-696` (`can_buy_weapon`) and `damage-life-state-03/decompiled/t6/_zm_perks.gsc:746-772` (`wait_for_player_to_take`).
- Trigger: press use to collect a box/Pack-a-Punch reward while reloading, especially just before expiry.
- Current behavior: any action other than `Ready` is busy, and the press is latched before that rejection. The player must finish the reload and release/repress use. `WeaponInventory::receive` already permits and cancels the reload states.
- Reference behavior: collection rejects drinking, mines/equipment, revive tools, and invalid players, but does not reject a normal gun reload.
- Impact: a weapon can expire in the draft during a collection that the original accepts.
- Correction direction: use transaction-specific purchase/collection eligibility; allow normal reload interruption when taking a replacement weapon.
- Tip status: still present.
