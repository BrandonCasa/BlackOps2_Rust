## Findings

### 1. [P1] Projectile splash applies enemy damage to the player

- Draft: `src/projectiles.rs:99-105`; integration: `src/equipment.rs:774-818`, `src/damage.rs:210-236`.
- Reference: `solo-content/source/weapons/ray_gun_zm:10` and `ray_gun_upgraded_zm:10`, native fields `playerDamage`, `minPlayerDamage`, `explosionInnerDamage`, and `explosionOuterDamage`. Staff definitions such as `staff_fire_zm:10` and `staff_revive_zm:10` explicitly have `playerDamage=0`.
- Firing the Ray Gun into nearby terrain creates a blast using 1500/300 enemy damage (2000/300 upgraded). The shared blast resolver applies those same values to every eligible target, including the shooter; the damage resolver has no projectile player-damage override. The native Ray Gun has `playerDamage=30`, distinct from its zombie damage. Thus even the draft's outer splash can immediately down a normally healthy player, and staff projectiles can damage their owner despite their zero player damage. Later elemental scaling further amplifies the staff case without fixing the separation.
- Correction: retain native player damage in prepared weapon mechanics and carry a separate player-damage calculation through projectile impacts and splash. Preserve enemy damage, original immunity, and perk protection independently.

### 2. [P2] Easy difficulty uses the next round's movement base

- Draft: `src/frontend.rs:26-31`; integration: `src/rounds.rs:342` and `RoundProfile::gait`.
- Reference: `mechanics-rounds-01/decompile/decompiled/t6/_zm.gsc:866`, `:903-909`, and `:3503-3508`.
- For normal progression after round one, the source updates movement using the round that is ending and then increments `level.round_number`. The existing Original profile accounts for this with `(round - 1) * multiplier`. The newly added Easy branch instead uses `round * 2`. Round two therefore gets base 4 instead of 2, and subsequent Easy rounds remain two units ahead, changing gait selection near each threshold and producing faster zombies earlier.
- Correction: preserve the source's round-one exception and use the preceding round index for subsequent Easy gait calculations. Distinguish normal fresh-session progression from any future nonstandard starting-round support.

### 3. [P1] Restoring a missing asset pack cannot be recovered by Retry loading

- Draft: `src/frontend.rs:206`, `src/assets.rs:370-380`, `src/assets.rs:560-566`.
- Integration evidence: `src/rendering.rs:97` runs `opaque::initialize` only at Startup; `src/rendering/opaque.rs:531-536` reads `map.gltf`, `:667-672` inserts `SurfaceLibrary` only on success. This is a project integration defect; no claim is made about recovered BO2 executable loading behavior.
- If the complete runtime pack or its surface metadata is absent on startup, the surface-library initializer fails without creating `SurfaceLibrary`. Restoring the pack and selecting the new Retry action reruns map loading but never reruns that initializer. Even if both scenes and their colliders subsequently load, `finish_load` cannot advance without the library and ultimately reports another timeout. The visible recovery action therefore fails for a principal missing-content case it is meant to address.
- Correction: make retry rebuild all pack-dependent loading state, including the surface library and failed dependencies, and reset any relevant preparation counters rather than only respawning map roots.

### 4. [P2] Projectile flight omits the native trail and looping audio

- Draft: `src/projectiles.rs:15-28`, `:77-80`, `:99-109`.
- Reference: `solo-content/source/weapons/ray_gun_zm:10`, native `projTrailEffect=misc/fx_trail_raygun_geotrail`, `projectileSound=wpn_rgun_loop`, and `projExplosionEffect=misc/fx_exp_raygun_impact`; the upgraded definition selects its own trail and impact effects. The Fire Staff definition similarly supplies a projectile loop and authored bolt trail.
- The newly spawned projectile contains only gameplay data and a transform. No renderer or audio system consumes `WeaponProjectile` to display its flight or play its loop. Impacts use the shared generic blast presentation. Projectiles therefore travel invisibly and silently between the muzzle sound and impact, losing visible travel, element identity, and useful trajectory feedback present in the reference assets.
- Correction: connect native projectile presentation and lifecycle audio to the reusable projectile entity, including normal retirement on impact, expiry, and session teardown. Reuse recovered assets rather than substitute newly authored effects.
