### FIRE-1 — P2 — Leaving the Crazy Place preserves corpses that the native puzzle discards

- Original code: `src/staff_puzzles.rs:253-264` stores anonymous countdowns and removes them only when burned or evicted by the 15-entry cap; it has no chamber-presence cleanup.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_quest_fire.gsc:54-59,90-106,196-219,234-269` maintains the clone queue, requires chamber occupancy for collection, and polls every second to delete all queued clones when the chamber is unoccupied.
- Trigger: kill zombies on an unlit sacrifice floor, leave the Crazy Place long enough for the native one-second absence cleanup, return later, and ignite the floor.
- Impact: all retained ready timers can still increase `sacrifices` even though the native pending corpses would have been deleted. The player can bank progress across departures without maintaining the source puzzle's presence/cleanup conditions; old real corpses may already have despawned independently.
- Future correction: retain reusable corpse/volume state and clear pending sacrifices on the recovered occupancy rule, with the native polling cadence. Do not clear already received sacrifices.
- Status at cutoff: outstanding. Later bounded commits do not change the Fire queue or add chamber-presence handling.

### FIRE-2 — P2 — Every explosion refreshes the ignition window and stage completion skips the native window boundary

- Original code: `src/staff_puzzles.rs:235,239-241,260-264` sets `ignition = 6.` on every qualifying impact, burns ready timers whenever it is positive, and immediately sets `first[0]` after sacrifice 32.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_quest_fire.gsc:136-174` turns the flame on, waits six seconds without listening for another explosion, then resumes the loop or emits `fire_sacrifice_completed`; `:62-79` waits for that notification before completing the first stage. The corpse callback can please the gods during that wait (`:260-267`).
- Trigger: fire another shot during an already active six-second window, or receive sacrifice 32 early in that window and immediately try church torches.
- Impact: sustained impacts extend one continuous burn beyond the source window and allow corpses that mature after the original window would have ended to burn without another postwindow ignition. The draft also reveals symbols/enables church processing as soon as the count reaches 32, potentially several seconds before the native first-stage transition.
- Future correction: model the active six-second ignition as a state that ignores retriggers until its boundary, then emit the source completion transition at that boundary when the required count is reached. Keep the sacrifice counter separate from first-stage completion.
- Status at cutoff: outstanding.

### FIRE-3 — P2 — Church torches have no native two-second input debounce

- Original code: `src/staff_puzzles.rs:242-250` processes each impact for every nearby torch and resets its timer to 15 seconds immediately, with no per-torch cooldown.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_quest_fire.gsc:414-440,443-470`: each torch listens for an explosion, starts/restarts its 15-second flame, then its listener waits two seconds before it can react again. `:447-459` uses the recovered 64-native-unit impact radius.
- Trigger: multiple Fire Staff projectiles land within 64 native units of one church torch less than two seconds apart, including a burst landing at the same target.
- Impact: the draft refreshes and emits ignition audio for every impact that the original listener would ignore. Correct and incorrect torches can therefore remain lit longer, and the simultaneous-set condition sees a different timing history. The 1.6256-meter radius and individual 15-second timers themselves match the source conversion.
- Future correction: add a per-torch two-second reaction deadline and refresh the 15-second flame only on accepted impacts; retain independent deadlines for different torches.
- Status at cutoff: outstanding.
