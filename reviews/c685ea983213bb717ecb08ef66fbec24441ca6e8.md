### P2 — Exchanging staffs resets their magazine and raises stock back to starting ammo

- Draft: `src/weapons.rs:287-292`; call at `src/crafting.rs:150-153`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:1133-1153` (`tomb_check_crafted_weapon_persistence`) and `:1243-1277` (`track_staff_weapon_respawn`).
- Trigger: fire a staff, take a different crafted staff, and later take the first staff again.
- Current behavior: deletes all staff slots and then calls normal `receive`/`grant`. `grant` creates a full magazine and sets stock to at least the definition's starting reserve; no per-staff saved clip/stock state exists.
- Reference behavior: tracks the previous magazine and reserve for each base/upgraded staff and restores them on pedestal collection.
- Impact: pedestal exchanges provide repeatable free ammunition and erase the staff's prior state. Later upgraded-staff ownership would also be downgraded by hard-coded base IDs.
- Correction direction: retain each staff's form, magazine, reserve, and owner/pedestal state independently of the active weapon slots, and restore that state on collection.
- Tip status: the tip still removes staff slots and receives a fresh definition. The later alternate-mode restoration does not preserve pedestal ammo.

### P2 — Gems bypass their matching portal reveal and crystal-specific recovery rules

- Draft: `src/crafting.rs:57-61`, `:84-87`, `:100-108`; gem sites at `src/crafting/source.rs:14-17`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_main_quest.gsc:551-609` (`staff_crystal_wait_for_teleport`); `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:551-604` (elemental crystal drop callbacks), `:680-707` (player crystal identity); `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_craftables.gsc:308-336`, `:633-646` (bleedout/disconnect drop lifecycle).
- Trigger: enter the shared Crazy Place through one element, walk to the other elemental gem locations, or bleed out/disconnect while carrying an uncrafted gem.
- Current behavior at this revision: all four gem targets are revealed at session setup and use the generic part pickup path. There is no per-element portal prerequisite, crystal identity/drop callback, or recovery of the despawned pickup.
- Reference behavior: each gem remains hidden until a player teleports through that element, then its plinth rises and the gem reveals. Carried crystals have explicit drop/reset behavior.
- Impact: one route grants unrelated gems prematurely, and crystal carrying has different failure/recovery rules.
- Correction direction: connect each gem's reveal to its own element transition and implement the crystal-specific carried/drop lifecycle rather than treating it as an ordinary shared permanent part.
- Later status: `97a587c0b` adds per-element `portals.visited` filtering and `recover_crystals`; the filtering addresses cross-element immediate availability, but recovery is only approximate. Its `health.state() != Standing` rule also returns the gem on entering last stand, whereas the recovered generic part watcher waits for `bled_out` (despite its `player_drop_piece_on_downed` name) or disconnect. The generic watcher selects a safe/current-player drop position before calling `piece.ondrop`, but the crystal-specific callback then moves the model back to its original plinth (`zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:544-556`); resetting a crystal to that plinth is therefore not itself a mismatch. The tip also reveals the pickup immediately after a visit without the recovered plinth rise/look sequence. Premature recovery and emergence timing remain to refine.

### P2 — Crafting never enters the source builder action or reserves the player's hands

- Draft: `src/crafting.rs:98`, `:129-142`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_craftables.gsc:1878-1940` (`craftable_use_hold_think_internal`).
- Trigger: hold use at a complete recipe while moving, firing, switching weapons, or interacting with other hand actions.
- Current behavior: accumulates three seconds whenever the gun happens to be `Ready`. It does not equip a builder action, suppress normal weapon actions, reserve the player's hands, or run action restoration on cancellation. The progress counter merely resets if another action later makes the gun busy.
- Reference behavior: disables player move states, increments the drinking/action lock, equips `zombie_builder_zm`, reserves the pieces, and restores the previous weapon and locks on either completion or failure.
- Impact: the visual and gameplay vulnerability of crafting are absent; players can interleave ordinary actions with the construction rather than entering the original uninterrupted builder action.
- Correction direction: use a reusable temporary hand action with deterministic cancellation/restoration, and reserve the recipe until it succeeds or is cancelled. Preserve the source three-second duration.
- Tip status: still present.

### P2 — Consumed ice staff pieces become eligible for another snow dig

- Draft: `src/crafting.rs:136`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_craftables.gsc:1315-1383` tracks pieces as crafted/crafting rather than making them absent; `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_craftables.gsc:274-291` installs the one set of ice staff pieces.
- Trigger: craft the ice staff, then dig another eligible mound during snow.
- Current behavior at this revision: removes consumed piece IDs from `Parts.pieces`. Existing digging code at `src/environment/digging.rs:415-420` and `:760-766` uses presence in that map to prevent a staff piece from spawning again. `Crafting.consumed` is not consulted by digging.

- Impact: later digs can produce duplicate spent staff pieces and displace ordinary dig rewards.
- Correction direction: retain a terminal consumed state in the shared part lifecycle and make all producers recognize it.
- Later status: fixed by `006dc2e7f88cc1dad9734de47a7bf2adbc1f5a7a`, which adds `PartState::Consumed` and inserts that state instead of removing the key. Do not apply a duplicate fix at tip.
