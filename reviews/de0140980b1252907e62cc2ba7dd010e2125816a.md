## Findings

### P2 - Require one uninterrupted complete tank circuit, not two independently banked legs

- **Code:** `src/achievements.rs:7-8,32-37`; integration in `src/tank.rs:96-108` and `src/movement/platforms.rs:35-39,67-86`.
- **Trigger:** Ride the first leg to its stop, leave the tank during cooldown, then board again for the return leg. Alternatively complete the two directions on separate circuits in the same session.
- **Draft behavior:** Each successfully ridden leg is inserted into `state.legs` and remains there until session reset. Going off the tank only invalidates the currently moving leg; it never clears previously banked legs. On the next departure, `riding` is set afresh from the current support, and having both station indices grants the achievement. Leaving during a cooling stop is therefore free.
- **Reference:** `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_tank.gsc:349-385` starts the watcher on boarding and notifies when the player leaves; `:404-421` ends that watcher on `player_jumped_off_tank` or death/disconnect, records one starting location, and waits through departures/stops until the tank returns to that same location. Boarding during travel first waits for that travel to stop. `zm_tomb_achievement.gsc:121-129` awards only that watcher's `rode_tank_around_map` event.
- **Impact:** I'm on a Tank! unlocks after two separate rides even though the player did not remain aboard for the full circuit. The discrepancy includes leaving and reboarding between the two normal route halves.
- **Future correction:** Track a single boarding episode, its eligible stationary starting location, and full return to that location. Invalidate the whole episode on leaving/death, including during cooldown; do not union station legs across unrelated rides.

### P2 - Include the solo self-release route for Kung Fu Grip

- **Code:** `src/achievements.rs:23-37`; integration in `src/panzer.rs:91-96`.
- **Trigger:** A solo player caught by a Panzer damages the claw/eligible release region and frees themself.
- **Draft behavior:** The achievement observer covers six IDs but has no Panzer release input or `ZM_DLC4_KUNG_FU_GRIP` award. Existing Panzer logic can release its captured owner after damage, but that transition never reaches achievement tracking. The commit's exclusion of cooperative-only conditions does not cover this self-release condition.
- **Reference:** `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_achievement.gsc:110-118` accepts either `mechz_grab_released_self` or `mechz_grab_released_friendly`; `zombies/_zm_ai_mechz_claw.gsc:79-86` explicitly emits the self event when releaser and captive are the same player. `zombies/_zm_ai_mechz.gsc:1513,1535` calls this helper from the release-damage paths. This is not contingent on another human player.
- **Impact:** A source-supported solo achievement remains impossible to earn locally despite the gameplay already having a self-release path and the checklist claiming solo-eligible conditions are tracked.
- **Future correction:** Emit a reusable attributed successful-release event from Panzer damage resolution and award this ID for the captured player's eligible self-release. Do not award for timeout, ordinary claw retraction, or unrelated target loss; keep friendly release conditional on cooperative support.

### P2 - Hide or clear achievement toast text when its gameplay session ends

- **Code:** `src/achievements.rs:14-15,19-24,39-43`; integration in `src/frontend.rs:195-205` and `src/session.rs:86-94`.
- **Trigger:** Earn an achievement and quit to the main menu or enter Game Over before the six-second notification expires.
- **Draft behavior:** `Toast` is a persistent UI entity without `SessionEntity`. Its text only refreshes while Playing, and its expiry only advances in the Playing fixed schedule. After teardown, the last earned message remains visible outside the gameplay session indefinitely. Even a PreparingShaders reset clears only the resource until the next Playing update rewrites the surviving text. The menu root occupies the center beginning at 15% screen height; the toast is at 8%, so it is not covered by that panel.
- **Reference:** `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_achievement.gsc:58-224` scopes achievement watchers to end-game/disconnect; `:17-27` provides a bounded achievement feedback action rather than a retained menu HUD string. Exact platform popup duration and layout are not reconstructed from this GSC, and no such timing constant is asserted. The definite defect is the new local toast retaining stale text after its owning session ends.
- **Impact:** A previous run's notification contaminates frontend/Game Over presentation and can outlive the achievement notification's intended lifetime.
- **Future correction:** Give the toast explicit state visibility/cleanup, update its text when reset or outside Playing, and retire its session presentation independently of persistent earned IDs.

### P2 - Queue simultaneous unlock notifications instead of overwriting them

- **Code:** `src/achievements.rs:20-21,28-31,41-43`; integration in `src/rituals.rs:49-50`.
- **Trigger:** Complete the sixth generator with no raid losses after already satisfying the headshot, spending, and soul-chest challenges. All Your Base and Overachiever then become eligible together or on adjacent fixed ticks depending on the order of the rewards observers.
- **Draft behavior:** Each `earn` persists its ID, then replaces the same `notification` string and resets its timer. The later unlock erases the first notification before meaningful presentation, although both IDs are already marked earned and will never notify again. This is reachable through normal solo gameplay rather than only an artificial bulk-unlock setup.
- **Reference:** `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_achievement.gsc:68-96` independently awards these two achievements from different completion events. `zm_tomb_challenges.gsc:26-42` defines the relevant thresholds; `zombies/_zm_challenges.gsc:246-288` completes the challenge and sends `all_challenges_complete` independently of the six-generator award. The source evidence establishes two distinct unlocks; exact native platform notification scheduling remains unavailable and is not assumed here.
- **Impact:** Local unlock persistence can report two successes while the player sees only the last, contrary to presenting each newly earned achievement. Depending on schedule order, the first is absent or flashes for only one fixed tick.
- **Future correction:** Enqueue unique newly earned notifications and display them serially with bounded durations. Preserve earned-ID deduplication without allowing one award to erase another's pending presentation.
