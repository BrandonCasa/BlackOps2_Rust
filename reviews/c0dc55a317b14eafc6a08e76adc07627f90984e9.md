## Findings

### 1. [P1] Every newly requested secret-song and story-log alias is absent from the runtime pack

- Draft: `src/side_secrets.rs:51-61`, `:68`; integration: `src/audio.rs:393-396`, `src/assets.rs:379-380`, `assets/origins/game.json:1`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_amb.gsc:432-443`, `:459-472`, `:614-618`; `zm_tomb_ee_side.gsc:650-654`; `zm_tomb_vo.gsc:836-860`. Native song aliases are also present in `audio-recovery/zm_tomb/soundbank/zmb_tomb.all.aliases.csv:4717-4719`; a native log alias is in `audio-recovery/en_zm_tomb/soundbank/zmb_tomb.english.aliases.csv:178`.
- The committed metadata contains none of `mus_zmb_secret_song`, `mus_zmb_secret_song_a7x`, `mus_zmb_secret_song_aether`, `zmb_meteor_activate`, `zmb_easteregg_scarydog`, or the entire `vox_maxi_audio_log_*` set. Loading uses this metadata without adding these aliases elsewhere, and the audio player silently discards requests with unknown keys. Completing an egg therefore consumes the interactions and blocks other song interactions for the hardcoded duration without playing music; reading a log marks it used while all of its segments are discarded. This is a wiring failure beyond the explicitly acknowledged missing radio props and jumpscare image.
- Correction: prepare the recovered alias/clip dependencies and register them in the runtime pack with their native channel, spatialization, and duration data. Validate the dependency closure before treating activation as successful. Do not replace the original sound content with invented audio.

### 3. [P2] Story logs become permanently unavailable after their first activation

- Draft: `src/side_secrets.rs:45`, `:54`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_vo.gsc:749-788`, especially unregistering only during playback at `:771` and registering again at `:788`.
- `state.logs[i]` is set immediately and the candidate filter excludes that log forever. The source keeps a separate discovered flag and restores the use trigger after the final segment. Players can therefore replay a completed recording in BO2; in the draft, an interrupted, inaudible, or completed recording cannot be replayed until the entire session resets. Retaining discovered progress across robot visits should not disable playback.
- Correction: separate discovery from per-log playback/interaction availability and restore use after that recording finishes or is interrupted according to the source lifecycle.

### 4. [P2] Starting another log discards the first recording's remaining segments

- Draft: `src/side_secrets.rs:54`, `:60-61`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_vo.gsc:761-779`.
- All six recordings share one `voice` queue, origin, and countdown. Activating a different log clears the pending segments and resets the countdown, while any already emitted clip keeps playing at its old origin. A player can start another recording before the first finishes, producing overlapping first clips and permanently losing the earlier queued content, compounded by the one-use filter. The source creates an independent sound origin and sequential completion-wait loop for each activated recording; it does not clear another recording's sequence.
- Correction: track playback per recording/origin with real completion handling. Preserve each sequence and use the corresponding per-log interaction lock rather than one globally replaceable queue.

### 5. [P2] Secret songs do not participate in the source music override

- Draft: `src/side_secrets.rs:51-57`, `:60`; integration: `src/audio/gameplay.rs:402-417`, `src/audio.rs:398-404`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_amb.gsc:459-472` sets the egg snapshot, stops stingers, and releases the override after playback.
- After the missing aliases are supplied, the draft's private `music` timer only gates other secret interactions. The regular audio systems keep emitting round-start/round-over music, and the retirement rule recognizes only the `mus_zombie_` prefix, excluding these `mus_zmb_secret_song*` aliases. A round transition during a song can therefore play competing music over it. The source explicitly stops stingers and applies its music snapshot for the song's lifetime. The draft also skips the source's one-second start delay.
- Correction: expose song playback/override state to the existing mixer and music-event producer, apply the source start and stop lifecycle, and restore ordinary music when playback ends or the session exits. Avoid using an isolated interaction timer as the audio authority.

### 6. [P2] Unactivated rock sites have no native looping discovery sound

- Draft: `src/side_secrets.rs:33-35`, `:51`, `:56`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_amb.gsc:432-439`; native `zmb_meteor_loop` is in `audio-recovery/zm_tomb/soundbank/zmb_tomb.all.aliases.csv:10318`.
- Setup creates only transform/interaction markers at the three source positions, and activation emits only a one-shot. Nothing starts or stops a rock-site loop, and the committed metadata contains neither that alias nor matching emitter entries. The source deliberately loops this audible clue until the site is used. Even after the song aliases are restored, approaching the hidden sites will omit the cue players use to discover the egg.
- Correction: prepare the original loop and attach one source-positioned emitter to each unused rock site, stopping it on that site's activation and during session cleanup.

### 7. [P3] The jumpscare requires an extra continuous aiming dwell at the target

- Draft: `src/side_secrets.rs:66-67`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_ee_side.gsc:243-253`.
- The source first counts 1.25 seconds of holding the ADS button, then checks the zone, sniper/iron-sight condition, and look direction. It does not require the player to spend that entire period facing the look point. The draft accumulates time only while all conditions, including the narrow view cone, remain true. A player already scoped for several seconds who sweeps onto the point triggers the source immediately at its next check, but must hold the exact direction for another 1.25 seconds in the draft; minor aim movement repeatedly resets progress.
- Correction: track the ADS-button hold independently and evaluate the source's remaining conditions once that hold threshold is reached. Keep the existing one-time session gate and bounded display duration.
