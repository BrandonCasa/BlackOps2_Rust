## P1 - Divider collider construction runs before its scene descendants exist

- Original-commit code: `src/chamber.rs:33-36`, with `Cargo.toml:35` and `src/lib.rs:205-208`.
- Reference assets: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_chamber.gsc:164-177` moves the actual chamber wall and connects/disconnects its paths after movement. `game-prepare/entities/maps/mp/zm_tomb.d3dbsp.ents:482-523` defines the four original `p6_zm_tm_elm_divider` script models. The prepared native model is `complete-assets-20261010/pack/library/models/zone/all/zm_tomb/f9817b0fba952a9788ff05e2/p6_zm_tm_elm_divider_lod0.gltf:1` (`nodes[12..14]` identify the mesh descendants and scene root).
- Supporting pinned dependency evidence: Avian revision `34c9b008d2f40638a953d9218db48d54f661365d`, `src/collision/collider/backend.rs:238-244,329-354,440-442`, registers hierarchy construction in `Update`, gates its scene-readiness wait on Avian's `bevy_scene` feature, and unconditionally removes `ColliderConstructorHierarchy` after iterating the current descendants. Its `crates/avian3d/Cargo.toml:45-46` does not enable `bevy_scene` through `collider-from-mesh`. Bevy 0.20.0 `bevy_app/src/main_schedule.rs:224-230` runs `Update` before `SpawnScene`; `bevy_world_serialization/src/lib.rs:64-68` spawns the world instances in `SpawnScene`. These sources were read from the local pinned Cargo caches.
- Trigger: normal session setup spawns each fresh divider root with `WorldAssetRoot` and `ColliderConstructorHierarchy` simultaneously. It has no children yet when the constructor first runs. Avian's `bevy_scene` feature is absent from this package's explicitly selected features, even though Bevy itself has scene support.
- Actual behavior: hierarchy construction sees no mesh descendants, removes its component, and is never retried when the scene's meshes arrive later. There is no other divider collider-construction path; `assets::scene_ready` only handles entities bearing `MapRoot`.
- Gameplay impact: the moving divider visuals have no corresponding new solid collision, and the chamber navigation refresh cannot discover their intended blockage. The central chamber hazard can be walked through rather than dividing the arena.
- Future correction: make collider construction wait for the divider's actual world-instance readiness, using the pinned Avian scene feature or an explicit ready observer, and require the constructed colliders before treating the walls as ready. Validate the moving child colliders against the native placements afterward.
- Status at the bounded tip: **outstanding**. `src/chamber.rs`, `src/navigation.rs`, and `Cargo.toml` are unchanged from this commit through b9af42289; no later commit in this pass remedies the missing readiness integration.

## P2 - A player standing on a navigation node becomes a persistent wall exclusion

- Original-commit code: `src/navigation.rs:96-111,256-257`; refresh is called by `src/chamber.rs:62-64`. Relevant surrounding integration is `src/navigation.rs:495-513`, which already excludes the player, actors, and temporary blockers from initial geometry admission, and `src/player.rs:110`, which supplies the player's solid capsule.
- Reference assets: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_chamber.gsc:164-177` changes connectivity on the wall entities themselves after their one-second moves. `game-prepare/entities/maps/mp/zm_tomb.d3dbsp.ents:482-523` identifies those wall models; `:30408-30413` supplies the 1800-source-unit chamber radius. The source does not make a standing player a chamber-wall path disconnector.
- Trigger: the 1.1-second post-movement refresh occurs while the player stands over a nearby native node or crosses one of its links. A node ray starts 0.8 metres above its feet, inside the standing player's capsule when the node is occupied. Zombies or other solid actors on a link can cause the same effect.
- Actual behavior: `solids([])` excludes no entities and the ray uses `solid = true`, so the actor is accepted as a wall obstruction, including a zero-distance hit when the ray starts inside the capsule. The result is written to `chamber_blocked` and subsequently suppresses the edge in `allowed`; moving the actor away does not remove it. Only a later wall refresh or session reset clears it. The pinned Avian `src/spatial_query/system_param.rs:88` explicitly defines the inside-origin behavior of `solid` rays.
- Gameplay impact: otherwise open chamber routes and spawn-admission connectivity disappear for the remainder of the current 5-15-second wall interval. If the chamber becomes unoccupied, the stale exclusion can remain until a later occupied wall change. Zombies may fail to route toward the player even after the physical link is clear. This defect is independent of the missing divider colliders above: player collisions alone can populate the exclusion set.
- Future correction: base these exclusions on the moved divider colliders, or at minimum exclude all actors and temporary gameplay blockers consistently with initial navigation admission. Reconnect paths from the wall state rather than cache transient occupancy as geometry.
- Status at the bounded tip: **outstanding**; the affected navigation and chamber functions have no changes in the remaining seven commits.
