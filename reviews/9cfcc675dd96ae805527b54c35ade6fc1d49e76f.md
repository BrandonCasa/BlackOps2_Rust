## P2 - Iron Fists bypass the normal melee action and permit simultaneous gunfire

- Draft: `src/rituals.rs:110-129`, scheduled before gameplay combat at `:35`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_one_inch_punch.gsc:96-141` monitors an actual player melee animation and waits for it to finish before accepting another swipe. `solo-content/source/weapons/one_inch_punch_zm` defines `meleeDelay=0.05`, `meleeTime=0.8`, `meleeChargeTime=0.85` (flat weapon-definition fields).
- Trigger: hold fire and press melee after claiming One Inch Punch, or start a reload/switch immediately after pressing melee.
- Actual: punches consumes only TickActions.melee, applies damage immediately, and leaves WeaponInventory.action Ready. The subsequent gameplay combat system can fire in the same tick; reload and switching are not blocked by the punch animation. The 0.8-second private cooldown merely rejects another punch.
- Impact: punch/gun damage can overlap and source melee recovery restrictions disappear.
- Future correction: integrate fists into the reusable melee action timing and animation/message path, with the punch area effect emitted at the appropriate hit point.

## P2 - Punch targeting is substantially wider and longer than the source cone

- Draft: `src/rituals.rs:122-128`.
- Reference: `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zombies/_zm_weap_one_inch_punch.gsc:124-136,146-157` first selects zombies within 100 source units and uses a yaw-only angle difference below 35 degrees; full damage uses squared distance 4096 (64 units). An upgraded Air punch doubles that full-damage squared-distance threshold at `:121-122`.
- Trigger: punch a zombie 40-60 degrees to the side or 100-128 source units away; for the future Air-upgrade path, punch between 64 and sqrt(8192) units away.
- Actual: the outer radius is 3.2512 meters (128 units), the normalized 3D dot threshold 0.5 allows a roughly 60-degree cone, and every element retains the same 64-unit full-damage radius.
- Impact: many lateral/distant targets are hit that the source excludes, while upgraded Air targets lose the source's larger full-damage radius. Height also influences eligibility differently from the yaw-only cone.
- Future correction: preserve the source outer selection range, yaw test and element-dependent squared-distance threshold explicitly. Air-upgrade accessibility itself is not implemented by this commit.
