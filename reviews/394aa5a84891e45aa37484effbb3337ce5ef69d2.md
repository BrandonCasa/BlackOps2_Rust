### P1 - The newly visible tank uses a native +X model with a -Z carrier and an undersized support collider

- **Original code:** `src/tank.rs:36-44,78-90`; `src/tank/source.rs:1-3`.
- **Reference:** `game-prepare/entities/maps/mp/zm_tomb.d3dbsp.ents:3105-3115` supplies the `tank_start` node with yaw 14.2; `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_tank.gsc:585-590` attaches the vehicle to that original path. Native forward is used in its tank-relative checks at `:1595-1596`. The now-selected `assets/origins/library/models/zone/all/zm_tomb/637309077daedb839c8181d5/veh_t6_dlc_mkiv_tank_lod0.gltf:1` has no outer corrective rotation and POSITION bounds X `[-4.140027,6.857090]`, Y `[-0.031121,2.982786]`, Z `[-3.066700,3.066700]`, approximately 11 by 3 by 6.13 metres with length along X. Its matched SHA256 is `de7d15ced77c2bb7f73745d4b1182ef6a20f9f2b201b9cd4d1ea53e601866575`.
- **Trigger:** Board the newly rendered tank, approach its visible ends/edges, or watch its first movement away from the church stop.
- **Current behavior and impact:** The body rotation is calculated so local -Z points down the route, and the native scene is parented with only a Y offset. The native tank's longitudinal +X axis is consequently sideways to the route and to the code's front-crush test. Its visible length/width also greatly exceed the `Collider::cuboid(3.,2.,6.)` carrier. At reset its root has identity rotation, which loses the start-node heading until movement first turns it. A player can stand over visible tank geometry without actual platform support, fall through a deck edge, or be blocked/damaged at a location that disagrees with the tank hull. This directly affects riding and the Lightning Staff jump sequence. Previous tank code already contained the approximate carrier; successfully binding the original scene exposes a concrete geometry/axis mismatch instead of resolving it.
- **Future correction:** Use a source-correct vehicle/model frame, set the stop heading at initialization, and align support/runover geometry with that same frame and native hull/deck. Preserve the imported metre conversion; do not add a second 0.0254 scale. Exact native vehicle collision hulls and suspension remain evidence gaps, but a 3-by-6 carrier is not aligned with this 11-by-6 native visual.

### P3 - All four recovered soul chests lose their original map rotations

- **Original code:** `src/rituals.rs:38-43`; `src/rituals/source.rs:1-5` retains positions and trigger names but no angles.
- **Reference:** `game-prepare/entities/maps/mp/zm_tomb.d3dbsp.ents:29660-29702` supplies the four `foot_box` models at these same converted positions with native yaws 94.8, 355, 180, and 170 degrees. `todo-audit-20261010/decompiled/t6/zm_tomb_challenges.gsc:59-60,124-134` uses those existing entities and preserves their starting angles during the completion sequence. - **Trigger:** Observe any newly visible soul chest, particularly the first chest with its approximately quarter-turn original orientation.
- **Current behavior and impact:** Each entity receives only `Transform::from_translation`, so every native scene faces the same world direction. The glTF does not contain each map placement's yaw. This is a presentation fidelity defect made observable by the successful binding; it does not change the preserved soul trigger coordinates by itself.
- **Future correction:** Carry the original entity rotations alongside positions and apply the repository's existing native-to-Bevy angle conversion. Keep completion/reset transforms anchored to that starting pose.
