# Rendering

Bevy 0.20.0-rc.2 owns the raster renderer: clustered lights, visibility, cascades, point shadow cubes, Gaussian PCF, native skinning, GPU preprocessing, material batching, atmosphere, environment-map filtering, SSAO and post-processing. The application no longer requests hardware ray-tracing features. Solari, runtime DDGI, traced local reflections, ray mesh/skin/material providers, GPU ray texture feedback, the custom shadow-cache scheduler and PCSS shader injection are removed. Their previous implementations and research remain in Git history.

## Lighting and shadows

`config/origins-visual.json` holds artistic settings and admission limits. The sun uses three native 4096-square cascades over 100 meters, native contact shadows and Gaussian filtering. Local lights use native 512-square cube faces and Gaussian filtering without PCSS. At most eight nearby fixture lights are active, chosen within 28 meters with a retention preference at distance-rank boundaries. Departing lights fade over 0.3 seconds with shadows intact; replacement lights wait for free slots. A six-meter distance fade smooths the outer boundary. Disabled fixtures retain their emissive material glow. These are tunable distance/detail approximations: far fixtures do not illuminate surfaces, and the budget can omit a visible light in a dense room. No unshadowed substitute leaks through walls.

Bevy's physical atmosphere supplies the visible sky and a 128-square environment cube. Native environment-map diffuse and specular lighting plus High SSAO replace dynamic ray transport. The atmosphere environment intensity is 0.08; a neutral 30-unit ambient fill preserves readability. This approximates indirect lighting: there is no dynamic color bounce or local scene reflection, and environment light does not resolve room occlusion beyond material AO and screen-space occlusion. Distance fog replaces the custom height-density volume and its shadowed ray-marched sun shafts. Native atmospheric aerial perspective is retained without shader replacement.

The approach follows [Bevy's native PCF overview](https://bevy.org/news/bevy-0-12/#pcf-shadow-filtering) and [contact-shadow example](https://bevy.org/examples/3d-rendering/contact-shadows/). The pinned engine source defines Gaussian as the stable nine-sample Castaño filter for directional/spot maps and supplies its point-light filter. SSAO is indirect contact occlusion, not a substitute for direct-light shadow maps.

## Materials, assets and presentation

`opaque.rs` extends StandardMaterial only for authored terrain layers, tile/coating masks and animated water. `pbr.rs` restores canonical prepared maps on standard skeletal/equipment materials. Version-two DDS channels retain full material-aware mip chains, source samplers, coverage and UV/normal transforms. Native lightmaps, per-instance irradiance and captured reflection cubes remain excluded from the runtime pack; originals are untouched. GGX fitting and material classification are reconstruction approximations.

Texture streaming retains native Image IDs and full source mip chains. Camera detail, first-person detail, shadow coverage and movement prefetch share a device-budgeted allocator; the deleted ray producers no longer request offscreen lighting textures. The budget accounts for bootstrap, pending and retiring GPU allocations. Native image/material preparation applies changes in order and completes GPU retirement before returning allocation credit. Extended materials use the pinned engine's native bindless implementation.

Both cameras compose into one linear HDR target. The first-person view retains its own depth attachment so the weapon cannot intersect world depth. The final camera applies AgX, native histogram auto-exposure, color grading, bloom and SMAA once. Native lights illuminate world and first-person layers; native material geometry, alpha coverage and animated poses provide their shadow casters.

## Dependency patches and validation

All Bevy crates come directly from the pinned registry versions; there are no vendored engine sources or Cargo patch overrides. The authored material vertex shader calls native transform, skinning and morph helpers directly; it does not depend on the deleted private `mesh_vertex` patch. Private frame-attribution APIs and their custom collector are removed. Application frame intervals remain measurable, and native development diagnostics/profilers remain available without pretending asynchronous pass samples have frame attribution. Shipping builds compile out logging/tracing and forbid profiling features.

Use optimized development builds (`tools/build.ps1`) and the actual game for routine acceptance. Use static full-LTO release builds and guarded repeated moving/combat workloads for performance claims. Ray-dispatch/cache counters are removed from receipts; raster method, active point-shadow counts, native caster admission, material/streaming errors and actual screenshots describe the replacement. Native admission alone does not prove receiver shadow quality. The [current handoff](../handoffs/current.md) records the build, visual and performance evidence separately.
