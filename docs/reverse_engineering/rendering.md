# Origins rendering

Bevy owns scenes, transforms, visibility, PBR, lights, shadows, cameras, filtering and post-processing. `src/rendering/opaque.rs` extends StandardMaterial with fresh embedded project-owned `opaque.wgsl`, `surface_vertex.wgsl` and shared `surface_bindings.wgsl`. The vertex adapter calls Bevy's mesh function and forwards authored layer attributes; shadow/depth passes retain Bevy layouts. Original shaders and captures are research references, never production inputs. No retired renderer, additional pipeline or viewer is present.

## First-person positioning

Offline Ghidra analysis of the installed Zombies executable establishes camera-relative weapon assembly (005332e0/00539990), hip-only FOV positioning bias (00533090), separate animated tag_camera eye composition (004a0730), linear clamped ADS transition fractions (008e1500/008e7b00), endpoint-dependent horizontal zoom (004a0890/005303e0) and reference 4:3 projection (004a10a0). Native axes are X-forward/Y-lateral/Z-up; preparation retains parent-local animation deltas and converts inches to meters. The hand and weapon wrappers perform the remaining glTF/Bevy conversion. Shared ADS applies the recovered torso trajectory after action sampling, while the group inversely compensates the animated eye so camera motion is applied once. Projection changes precede Bevy camera updates and audio positioning follows the final eye.

World and viewmodel lenses agree at full ADS; retained hip lenses and the 25 mm upward hip framing remain reconstruction and fade out of aiming. Native look-lag suppression reaches zero at half ADS. A bounded correction below two degrees rotates the authored sight geometry onto the aiming ray for the five currently exercised base weapons. The prepared optical point uses the actual rigid skin joint and inverse bind, rather than treating every tag_sights socket as an optical center. This correction, reverse ADS-up sampling on exit, procedural motion and omitted variable-zoom/attachment branches are explicit approximations. Decompiled originals, hashes and function exports remain external; rendered results and scope exclusions belong in the current handoff. Refresh assembled positioning metadata with tools/prepare_origins.py --prepare-ads before validating/importing its complete asset closure.

## Terrain and surface response

Preparation decodes retained half-float secondary UVs and packed signed normal-basis rotations. Four registered glTF attributes carry them: `_ORIGINS_LAYER_UV`, `_ORIGINS_LAYER3_UV`, `_ORIGINS_ROTATION1`, `_ORIGINS_ROTATION2`. They follow the same expanded corners as normals, tangents, lightmap UVs and RGBA controls. Source world triangles are clockwise relative to their normals; preparation reverses complete triangles to Bevy/glTF CCW. Source culling then preserves floors and walls. Static provider winding stays unchanged.

Readable `surface.layers` records base/blend/multiply/add operations, reveal parameters and control/basis interpretation. Binding hashes establish layer identity rather than texture-table order; sparse specular layers remain valid. Up to four colors and three normal/specular layers cover playable world combinations. Vertex G/B controls and overlay alpha form parameterized reveal masks; each resulting coverage also multiplies the overlay texture alpha, preserving fractional opacity for blood, grime and blend/multiply/add layers without making the base wall transparent. Secondary UVs sample matching overlays; packed 2x2 bases orient normal slopes. Blend weights combine normal/gloss/specular response. Multiply/add color overlays preserve the underlying response; a fourth color supplies macro modulation. Unrecovered reveal defaults are strength 0.25/exponent 1; the readable profile scales reveal width by 3.2 to soften hard decal transitions. This is a practical reconstruction.

`tile_macro_bake` retains the shared fourfold diffuse conversion, sampled once by Bevy. Normal binding 0 supplies whole-object macro shape at unscaled authored UVs. Detail normal bindings 1/2 supply coating/core at fourfold UVs: macro red weights coating, green weights core. Add the blended detail slopes to the macro slope; never tile the macro shape or substitute it for the core detail. Specular bindings 0/1 likewise follow coating/core order at detail UVs. The diffuse bake already retains mask occlusion, which must not multiply albedo again. Retained rubble bindings (`p6_rocks_cluster_01_n`, `rubble_concrete_dust01_n`, `concrete_wall_broken_n`) and pillar/monolith bindings establish these roles. Slope addition and fourfold detail repeats remain practical approximations. Static providers and placement references remain shared; UV/basis defaults attach to the existing mesh rather than copies per instance.

Raw linear normal RG uses slopes `4.015748 * sample - 2.015748`, with DirectX Y and prepared tangent handedness applied once. Raw specular RGB/gloss maps to squared RGB dielectric F0 and `pow(1-gloss, gloss_exponent)` roughness. Missing channels use zero slope, F0 0.04 and roughness 0.8. Metal-like tint remains a dielectric approximation; names do not infer metallicity.

## Special surfaces and water

Preparation reads retained TECH4/TECH2 blend, alpha-test and cull state. Explicit `surface.presentation` supplies alpha/cutoff, culling, emissive response, fog, visibility, collision suitability, wetness suitability and lamp disposition. Runtime blanket double-sided rendering and material-name substring guesses are removed. Legitimate uncullable surfaces remain two-sided; fences/foliage use cutouts. Tree-canopy transparency deliberately becomes a cutout, with reconstructed cutoff 0.5. Add/multiply/premultiplied/blend states use matching Bevy modes, including ONE/INV_SRC_ALPHA and ZERO/SRC_COLOR dispositions. Freshly lit premultiplied surfaces supply alpha-weighted radiance after fog; the decoded source DDS albedo itself is straight color. Separate static phong/emissive bindings supply glow instead of becoming whole-object albedo. Four explicit lamp identities place warm Bevy lights; other glowing objects do not become lamps.

Shadow/editor-default helpers are visually hidden. Nonsolid static water/unlit/effect surfaces are excluded from collision. The giant smoke-pillar atmosphere proxy is explicitly hidden: its native multistage effect is not reconstructed, and its previously solid rectangular planes duplicated fog and covered the sky. Single Bevy distance fog replaces that volume approximation.

Water uses both retained normal maps at different moving UVs, F0 0.02, adjustable tint/opacity/roughness, Bevy transparency and assigned filtered source reflection cubes. Authored water UVs span hundreds of repeats, so a reconstructed 0.02 scale makes the retained normal patterns visible as meter-scale ripples rather than subpixel noise. Real presentation time continues during pause; fixed-step gameplay is unchanged. One shared GPU animation clock updates; water material assets and their bindings remain unchanged. Colored transparency reveals underlying geometry as simplified shallow refraction; no screen-space scene copy or depth distortion is used. Static surroundings reflect through moving normals; live actors do not enter those cubes.

Wetness applies only to explicitly suitable opaque lit surfaces. Up-facing orientation and broad world-space patches scale wetness, modestly darken albedo, move F0 toward 0.04 and lower roughness. This approximates damp ground/stone without repainting walls, foliage, glow or water, or blending tile/macro controls into diffuse twice.

## Lighting and atmosphere profile

`config/origins-visual.json` is embedded at build time: edit and rebuild to tune. It loads no local paths or captures. Light/surface/fog RGB values are sRGB; the generated HDR sky palette is linear radiance scaled by `sky_nits`.

| Controls | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ambient/sun color, brightness/illuminance and rotation | Cool actors/unbaked surfaces; baked diffuse excludes ambient/sun |
| indoor/outdoor EV100, adaptation speed | Cover-ray exposure; higher EV darkens the view |
| baked/vertex/probe/tile probe/reflection radiance, instance probe fill | Separate atlas/vertex and SH calibration; tile/macro probe gain joins loose rubble to baked terrain; subtle fill for missing local lighting |
| reveal softness, transparent opacity scale, post saturation | Tunable blending, glass coverage and restrained color grade |
| antialiasing | Smaa is the default; Msaa4 restores four-sample MSAA |
| comparison FOV degrees | Opt-in capture projection; ordinary player camera is unchanged |
| normal/tile strength, gloss exponent, F0 scale | Continuous retained-channel response |
| bloom, fog RGB/density | AgX bloom and single exponential distance fog |
| lantern RGB/lumens/range, lamp/other emissive nits | Warm pools and glow; all 331 lamps retain shadows; shadow face resolution is tunable |
| water tint/opacity/roughness/normals/UV scale/velocities | Transparent animated water |
| wetness/darkening/roughness | Damp up-facing solid surfaces |
| sky palette/nits/sun direction | Procedural storm cube supplied to Bevy Skybox |

Ambient brightness falls from 180 to 35, sunlight from 2600 to 1600 lux, lanterns from 6000 to 2600 lumens, bloom from 0.18 to 0.14 and fog density from 0.009 to 0.006. Indoor/outdoor EV100 8.25/8.7 balances warm rooms and cool outdoor rubble. Bevy scales skybox radiance by camera exposure once. Continuous procedural storm clouds replace the plain clear background through standard Bevy Skybox, approximating the supplied reference sky.

Packed world atlases decode linear RGB/alpha ambient and directional radiance plus source-basis direction. Irradiance is `A + D * clamp(dot(L, mapped_normal), 0, 1)`, with band-local insets. Static instances interpolate retained LOD0 vertex colors or use assigned source-basis SH. Preparation already retains original LOD vertex identity for expanded provider corners. The vertex adapter samples a small per-instance RGBA16F lighting texture through that identity; providers and placements remain shared. Provider attributes are prepared once per mesh asset and reused after CPU mesh data is released, including restart/late scene instances. Residency releases only completed providers so a loaded mesh awaiting surface adaptation retains its CPU vertices. Alpha remains lighting metadata, never surface opacity. SH uses 60 radiance units for probe-only props and tile/macro surfaces, versus atlas 90 and vertex 140. Probe-only non-tile props retain at least 25 percent of their own spherical-mean SH irradiance to reconstruct missing local bounce; zero-energy probes remain dark. The spherical mean/fill is computed once per shared material into spare uniform RGB lanes; the fragment adds only a componentwise maximum. No extra texture samples, lights, or shadow maps are required; vertex lighting gets a modest 0.035 probe fill to retain readable colored faces in deep shade. These independent gains are reconstruction controls. In the spawn-room rubble, nearby up-facing world corners have median irradiance RGB approximately (80,147,183), while the assigned upward SH at gain 22 gives (32,52,63). Tile gain 60 gives (86,142,172), joining loose rocks to the cool baked terrain. Ordinary props now use the same gain after near-black radio cases exposed the earlier undercalibration; vertex-light gains remain unchanged. This calibrates a family of materials, not a screen-space region or native-equivalence claim. A material specialization excludes duplicate ambient/sun diffuse; the extension supplies baked radiance once. The pinned Bevy PBR module is adapted in memory to gate its five LIGHTMAP lighting conditions on ORIGINS_BAKED_LIGHTING. Its equations remain engine-owned, with a version guard. Converted authored materials remove fallback Lightmap components: dummy black per-entity lightmaps fragmented Bevy mesh binding allocations. Emission follows camera exposure (Bevy emissive exposure weight 1). Lamp emission is warm at 4,000 nits; other emissive regions use 150 nits so equipment gauges do not compete with the lanterns. Point lights illuminate baked surfaces; actors/unbaked meshes use ordinary profile lighting. Unsupported special families retain their standard fallback.

Assigned source cubes decode to unclipped linear RGBA16F. Bevy owns GGX filtering/mips. Retained immutable cubes are filtered once; render-world feedback confirms generator bindings and all five compute pipelines were ready before removing only the generator component. EnvironmentMapLight and material handles retain the filtered images. Procedural or changing cubes must not use this static-probe freeze path. WGSL rotates reflection directions to source Z-up, selects roughness mips and uses Bevy's split-sum response. Reflection enters scene-linear lighting once and preserves transparent alpha before fog/post-processing. Albedo is sRGB; normal/specular/control/lightmap/probe data are linear. No native intermediate square-root transfer or extra albedo squaring is reproduced. AgX, display transfer, exposure and bloom remain Bevy-owned. Runtime RGBA8 material images receive box-filtered mip levels and eightfold anisotropic sampling; color mips filter in linear light, while data/alpha remains linear. Packed lightmap atlases stay exempt, and Bevy continues to filter reflection cubes. These are reconstructed levels, not recovered original DDS mips.

## Rendering performance

The material extension uses Bevy bindless ExtendedMaterial allocation on supported adapters, with a non-bindless WGSL fallback. Binding indices 100..128, table binding 129, packed parameter array binding 130 and shared animation buffer array binding 132 avoid StandardMaterial indices. Each mesh uses Bevy's material slot, including assignment-specific vertex lighting and reflection cubes. No instance lighting or source texture is merged or discarded. Camera transforms and HUD text change only when their values differ. All 331 lanterns keep their shadow flags enabled. The former 10-meter switch is removed. NoFrustumCulling keeps the cube array and light slots stable through camera rotation; finite-range Bevy clustering still limits per-fragment contributions. SMAA replaces four-sample MSAA to reduce GPU bandwidth; its edge treatment differs and Msaa4 remains selectable. CPU mesh batching and CPU clustering avoid excessive per-shadow GPU work buffers and cluster-resize transitions. The embedded lantern_shadow_map_size defaults to 512 instead of 1024: this lowers all-lantern depth storage from about 7.8 GiB to 2.0 GiB at the cost of finer shadow detail. This is an explicit tunable quality tradeoff, separate from reuse of unchanged rendering work.

Bevy's documented profiler is Tracy. Build `--features profile-tracy` and use a client matching the Cargo-locked tracy-client-sys bundled protocol (currently Tracy 0.14.0). `tools/profile_rendering.ps1 -Output <fresh-external-folder> -TracyDirectory <matching-tools>` captures the actual `origins.exe` worst room view; `-AllViews` captures all fifteen; `-Combat` captures an uncapped six-kill/Round 2 workload (marker view 15). It sets an info-level filter so ECS spans exist, disables optional ETW sampling/online symbol resolution, preserves the prior environment, exports CPU and GPU zones separately and validates nested elapsed-time flamegraph accounting. Avoid combining Tracy and Chrome features for measurement. `profile-chrome` is an optional separate diagnostic build, not the validated delivery profile.

Normal releases contain no profiler instrumentation. `--benchmark <external-folder>` disables VSync for the paused fifteen-view survey; `--benchmark-view <folder> <index>` isolates a view. `--benchmark-moving-view <folder> <index>` continuously sweeps that camera for 66 virtual seconds, sampling 4..60 and then capturing twelve fixed directions outside the FPS interval. Static views sample 4..12 after settling. Receipts contain physical/logical resolution, native/profiling flags, real elapsed frame intervals, mean/percentiles, per-pass diagnostic measurements and actual camera records. A paused render survey does not claim combat validation: use `--verify <external-folder>` separately for movement, six kills, Round 2, fifteen views and restart. `--benchmark-combat <folder>` measures uncapped combat after two seconds of startup and then one settled room view. Zombie actors share immutable mesh/material assets; spawning or removing one no longer creates source assets and invalidates every lantern map. Material asset events invalidate only their caster users; modified geometry and shaders invalidate conservatively. Permanent engine shader errors fail verification rather than producing a false high-FPS success with missing draws.

Flamegraph widths are exclusive instrumented thread elapsed time reconstructed from nested Tracy CPU zones, including waits, not CPU stack sampling. Thread totals run concurrently and cannot be added as frame latency. Selected benchmark markers define an exact window; the exporter rejects negative durations, crossing zones and failed self-time conservation. GPU timestamp zones are a separate export and never folded into the CPU graph. Report native FPS separately because tracing many engine zones materially slows this scene. Loading, shader compilation warmup and screenshot readback are excluded from steady intervals; startup stutter remains outside those averages. Current measurements and failures belong in the current handoff.

Shadow reuse keys include Bevy texture identity/slot ownership, actual projection matrices, light range/bias/layers and caster revisions. Changed transforms, geometry, inherited visibility, materials, textures and shaders invalidate affected maps; removed casters invalidate their old bounds. Skinned actors invalidate conservatively. Sun cascades also reuse unchanged maps, with camera/projection changes forcing redraw. Engine phases and ExtractedView components are retained between frames but suspended from preparation/drawing when their maps are valid; a dirty face restores the normal engine view and resets its phase before queueing. Cube-face revisions use Bevy frusta against conservative transformed-box bounds, including both old and new positions. Cached lantern caster visibility is restored before extraction without manufacturing change ticks. The application does not substitute shadow passes or depth textures. `--uncached-shadows` after a view benchmark disables reuse for comparisons.

Mesh instance preprocessing now uses Bevy's GPU builder by default, with automatic CPU fallback on unsupported devices. Compute expands compact mesh inputs into render uniforms; Bevy visibility and direct batching remain active. Temporal occlusion culling remains disabled. Restored shadow phases use the same device/view mode as Bevy's light preparation; cached views remain suspended from compute and drawing until invalidated. Mesh transforms, skinning, materials, visibility and buffer lifetimes remain engine-owned. Benchmark receipts record the active mesh preprocessing mode and Bevy compute-pass timings. Append `--cpu-preprocessing` for the previous CPU path or `--gpu-culling` to compare Bevy's supported indirect/frustum culling path. These mutually exclusive benchmark options can be combined with `--uncached-shadows` in either order. Each selects matching instance building and phase batching. Benchmarks use continuous focused/unfocused window updates as well as disabled VSync, preventing background throttling from distorting throughput. Normal play retains Bevy's default focus policy. Runtime asset data, lighting, resolution and spatial antialiasing are unchanged. Actual window/image/measurement outcomes belong in the current handoff.

After immutable cube filtering and material assembly, Bevy RenderAssetUsages::RENDER_WORLD moves source texture/mesh CPU payloads to the renderer. Font atlases remain writable. Preparation arrays and unused source texture handles are released; runtime materials retain their required images. Collision-only Mesh3d/material components are removed after Avian shape creation, and static collider parenting is flattened while preserving the world transform. CPU mesh bytes are unavailable for later map reassembly; restart retains the assembled world. Hot reassembly would need asset reload before conversion/collider building.

## Fidelity and evidence

The render root stays hidden until material/lightmap assembly completes. The actual-window driver traverses combat/movement/round/restart, sweeps eight map positions/yaws, adds five room directions and repeats a stationary water view. Comparison captures hide player overlays and record actual camera position/yaw/pitch/FOV/aspect in the receipt. Compiler gates cannot validate WGSL; game logs and inspected captures are required. Asset checks establish integrity, never original equivalence. Current outcomes/failures belong in the single current handoff.

Comparison targets supplied room colors, warm lamps, cool rubble, vegetation, distant haze and overcast sky. Remaining approximations include reveal constants, native radiance/lobe transfer, SH/basis interpretation, shallow water refraction, static reflections, procedural sky, canopy cutoff and absent source mip chains. Original volumetric smoke/cloud animation and temporary actor/weapon replacement are not implemented. Lantern bloom approximates halos; distance fog does not reproduce the reference's localized drifting smoke pockets. The world-material milestone does not replace the temporary pistol, hands or diagnostic HUD.

The forward surface adapter requires RGBA vertex colors. Mesh conversion supplies neutral white only when the optional glTF color attribute is absent, preserving every authored color/alpha value. The vertex-color/overlay-alpha hotfix passed actual-window combat, Round 2, fifteen map views and restart with zero rendering errors; settled blood-stain views were inspected. Original texture alpha remains unchanged.
