## Correctness

`tools/verify_indirect_rendering.py` ran against the pinned bundle with explicit DX12 and Vulkan backends. DX12 passed 20 device/queue pixel-readback cases; Vulkan passed 26. Cases cover indexed/non-indexed draws, nonzero vertex/instance offsets, signed indexed base vertex, GPU-written zero/oversized counts, invalid arguments, zero capacity, destination-pool splitting, and direct draws after indirect commands. Source-window crossing cases passed on Vulkan; DX12 cannot allocate the required crossing buffer within its advertised maximum buffer size.

The full DX12 gameplay scenario passed normal movement (1.828 m), camera input (0.480 rad), firing (0 to 1 shots), active GPU culling, and four active shadow maps. Initial and rotated screenshots were reviewed for geometry. The earlier candidate's missing/displaced geometry was corrected by removing Bevy's separate DX12 counted-submission exclusion so GPU packing and submission agree. Both final validation games shut down normally. These are bounded checks, not a claim of exhaustive correctness.
